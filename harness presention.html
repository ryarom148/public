Lusser's Law (Law of Reliability): The overall reliability of a sequential chain is equal to the product of the reliability of its parts. Ten steps at 99% accuracy yield only ~90% overall success. The system is weaker than its weakest link and degrades rapidly with each additional step.

Without execution loops, self-checks, and verification gates, complex tasks fall apart.
